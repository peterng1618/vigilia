What actually moved

The four changes, none of them colour

  • The subject leads. A titled block naming the thing and what kind of thing it is — today the column opens with a chrome label, SELECTION.
  • Values read; controls are for changing. Mono, right-aligned, baseline-aligned to their label. Today every field is an identical bordered box, which is why it reads as a form.
  • Spends becomes scannable. Token → value rows. Today it dumps sentences, including the type presets as paragraphs.
  • One quiet rule instead of shouting. The gap-not-zero rule is stated once in a note. Today it is trailing prose after the token dump.

Deliberately not in this screen

  • Colour and palette. Held constant on purpose so you judge language alone.
  • The shell. Header, pane bar, column widths and the stage come next — they are the same language at a larger scale.
  • Density of the sections. Whether Content/Position/Layer are open or closed by default is a separate call.
  • The controls themselves — sliders, selects, the colour field — are the next question, not this one.
