How you know what you can change
CPU load▼ A value inside a well means editable. The well — its inset fill and radius — is what says "you can change this", not the border. A chevron means it opens a list; no chevron means you type or scrub it.
A bounded number gets a track. Opacity, scale and blur have an end; a slider shows where you are without a keyboard.
accent.cool #4da3ff No well means read-only. Spends has no border and no chevron anywhere in it, and it says so in its header. Nothing here is a target you can miss.
CPU load▼ Focus is an accent boundary and a quiet halo. Measure the solid boundary's contrast in every palette; retain a visible system outline in forced-colours mode.
